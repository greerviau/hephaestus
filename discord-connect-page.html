Where to find these

  1. On the Discord developer portal (opens in a new tab), create an application, open Bot, and reset and copy the token. Turn on Message Content Intent.
  2. Under OAuth2, URL Generator, tick bot, and the permissions View Channels, Send Messages, Create Public Threads, Send Messages in Threads, Read Message History, and Attach Files. Open the URL to invite the bot to your server.
  3. In Discord, turn on Developer Mode, then right-click your name for your user ID and each channel you will use for its ID.
